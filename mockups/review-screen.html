Review screen mock-up (slice S2)

Your picks from the options board: layout 4 (verdict bar and severity groups) in the Drawing office look, the part fading while a finding is selected, and a "Passed" list. Each frame is a 1280 px screen, so scroll sideways inside it on a phone, or use the screenshots at the top. The 3D view here is a drawing, not the real render.

What changes in the spec if you approve: the "Web page" layout line becomes "controls and part size in a bar on top, a verdict line under it, the 3D view on the left and the findings on the right in severity groups with a Passed group; below 900 px the findings stack under the view".

Behaviour: faces with a finding are coloured by severity and labelled with their id and severity word. Clicking a finding turns its faces the accent blue, fades the rest of the part, and frames the camera on them; clicking it again clears the selection. Changing the material keeps your view.

1. A medium finding selected (deep_hole.step)

F1 is selected: the hole turns blue, the rest of the part fades, and the camera frames the hole. The flag on the view repeats "F1 MEDIUM", so colour is never the only sign.

2. A high finding, nothing selected (very_deep_hole.step)

Before any click, every face with a finding is coloured by its severity and carries its label.

3. A hole-size finding (odd_hole.step)

The suggestion names the nearest drill. Hole depth passed, so it is listed under Passed.

4. Clean part (clean_holes.step)

No findings. The verdict and the Passed list show which rules ran and on how many holes.

5. Empty, loading and error

Empty: no file chosen yet.

Loading: the previous part stays, faded, until the new one arrives; the findings panel keeps its space.

Error: the same box shows each J7 message: "This is not a STEP file this tool can read", "No solid found in this file", "File is larger than 50 MB" and "This file took too long to analyse". The view and summary clear, and another file can be chosen at once.

6. Below 900 px (880 px frame)

Below 900 px the findings move under the view and the bar wraps. At 900 px and wider the two columns stay side by side.
