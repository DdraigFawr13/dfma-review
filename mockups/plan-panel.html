Machine plan panel

S5 adds a Machine plan panel under Findings. It shows the machine, the tool loads, and each setup with its tools and the features each tool cuts. These are screenshots of the built screen on the real test parts (LESSONS "ui": mock a built screen with the real viewer), not a sketch.

Your call

Approve, Change (say what), or Rethink.

One thing to know before you judge it: directions are the part's own axes, and +Z is the outward normal of its largest flat face (spec "Flow" step 5). When features are cut into the top, the bottom is the largest face, so the top setup reads "from −Z". The panel says this in its last line.

States

  • Empty (no part loaded) and error: the panel is hidden, so the screen is as before (web/src/main.ts, plan-panel.hidden).
  • Loading: the panel keeps the last part's plan until the new result arrives, as Findings does.
  • Turning: no mill plan, so the panel is hidden until S6 adds the lathe plan.
