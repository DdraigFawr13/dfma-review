Hole features (I2 S1): status

S1 is built and its checks pass. With an .ipt, the app now lists every Hole feature with its type and sizes as designed, maps each of its holes onto the STEP's holes (matched, disagrees or not mapped), and a disagreeing hole is a finding. The panel and PDF section are as you approved (D-040).

Nothing to decide now. Try it in the demo: pick the "NIST CTC 04 + its .ipt (lined up)" entry.

  • Tap hf-7: its holes light up and its 4 disagreeing holes unfold; tap one to show it alone. Each is also a MEDIUM finding (F35-F38).

Measured

  • Read, against the answer key (InventorLoader's output; 85 key Holes paired by position): type right on 85 of 85, diameter 85 of 85, depth 43 of 43 where the hole ends inside the part. 0 wrong.
  • Mapped on 7 NIST pairs (holes matched / disagree / not mapped): CTC 01 10/0/0, CTC 02 42/6/4, CTC 04 40/13/1, CTC 05 10/11/0, FTC 08 0/4/0, FTC 10 31/4/0, STC 10 17/7/0. The disagreements are real differences between NIST's two files (they come from two CAD systems), e.g. CTC 04's M12 holes are 25 mm long at Ø10.11 in the .ipt and 30 mm at Ø10.20 in the STEP.
  • Not yet measured: D-032's 95% matched is for a pair you export from Inventor yourself. Assumption until your work pair (from 2026-11-12) shows it.

Settled while building (in the spec)

  • A hole and its mirror on one axis are two holes (CTC 05: 21 holes).
  • A drill point cut short by a cavity leaves its hole open at the bottom (2 of 76 drill points).
  • Depth is compared on the full-diameter part of each hole, on both sides.
  • Bad data in the .ipt (broken tags, loops that never close) is skipped or shown as "not mapped", never a refusal; tested by rewriting a NIST file.
