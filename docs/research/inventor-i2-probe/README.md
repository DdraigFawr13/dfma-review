# I2 + I3 probe: hole features, names, fillets and chamfers (2026-11-09)

Throwaway research scripts, not app code, written from the bytes of the 16 NIST `.ipt` files (no InventorLoader or Autodesk code read). They feed `docs/specs/inventor-i2-i3-holes-fillets.md`. Run with the dfma env from this folder; `s11`, `s15`, `s19` need the NIST STEP zip; `s28`, `s29` read InventorLoader's output from the session scratchpad (`K`), now saved as `tests/inventor/holes_key.json`.

## Answer key (InventorLoader output only)

InventorLoader 1.5.1 (e94bdf5) in a scratch FreeCAD 1.0.0 env from conda-forge, GUI binary offscreen, `dump.py` as the macro (answers the "choose strategy" dialog with code 2). 91 `PartDesign::Hole` in 10 files (none in CTC 03, FTC 07 2021, FTC 08 2024, FTC 09, FTC 11). It gives no fillets or chamfers as features, so names for those have no key.

## Measured

- **Feature tags** (`geo.py`): every face of the main model carries attributes; Inventor 2021 names them `NMx_<kind>-…-attrib`, 2024 writes `ATTRIB_CUSTOM-attrib` with the kind as its first string (`INV_NMX_HOLE_TAG`, `INV_NMX_BLEND_TAG` …). After the version int (43 in 2021, 44 in 2024; 2024 has one more leading int) come pairs `(k, feature id)` up to `-1`. The last id of a hole face is its Hole feature; ids before it are the pattern or mirror features that copied it (FTC 08: `1997`, `2187 1997`, `2202 1997`, `2202 2187 1997`).
- **Topology** (`topo.py`, `s17.py`): face → loop (4th pointer) → first coedge (loop's 4th); coedge: next 3rd, partner 5th, edge 6th, loop 7th; edge: vertices 3rd and 4th pointer; loop's face its 5th; vertex → point (last pointer). Surfaces: `cone-surface` (origin, axis, major axis whose length is the radius; 2nd float the sine of the half-angle, 0 for a cylinder), `torus-surface` (centre, axis, major, minor radius), `sphere-surface` (centre, radius), `plane-surface` (point, normal). cm.
- **Hole instances** (`s10.py`): a Hole feature's cylinder and cone faces grouped by axis line. CTC 04: 9 features = InventorLoader's 9 Holes. Type and diameter from the faces agree with the key on 73 of 73 Hole features checked (`s29.py`); a designed drill point shows only when the hole ends inside the part (12 depth-defined holes come out through the part).
- **Against the STEP** (`s11.py` at 0.05 mm; `s11b.py` gives the states at 0.01 mm, as the spec): instances on a STEP hole axis within 0.05 mm: CTC 01 10/10, CTC 02 42/52, CTC 04 48/54, CTC 05 18/18, FTC 08 2021 4/4, FTC 10 2021 34/35, STC 10 22/24 (each hole under its Hole feature, copies included). The misses lie 0.07-8.13 mm off (the two NIST files differ). FTC 06 (8/18, 4/12) and FTC 07 2024 (0/12) are not lined up by the harness (corner hits `s12.py`: FTC 07 52/346).
- **Names** (`s25.py`, `s28.py`): Hole names are UTF-16 `Hole<n>` text in the smallest `RSeStorage/B` stream with an `M` table that holds any. Where the count of Hole feature ids equals the count of names (8 files), the k-th smallest id has the k-th name by number: 70 of 70 checkable Holes right, 0 wrong, 3 not checkable. FTC 06 2024 (4 ids, 7 names) and STC 10 (9, 11) get none.
- **Blends** (`s14.py`, `s15.py`, `s17.py`): `blend` tags mark fillets and chamfers alike. A fillet's faces are cylinders, tori and spheres of one radius (spline faces carry none); a chamfer's are planes and cones. Fillet faces that sit on a STEP face of their type and place have its radius (499 of 744 checked on 11 pairs; 244 misses have no STEP face of their type there; 1, on FTC 11, has a STEP torus at its place with other radii, not yet explained). Chamfer distances from the neighbours: CTC 01 50x50 and 5x5, CTC 04 5x5, CTC 05 6.35x6.35 mm. Counts equal the `Fillet<n>` / `Chamfer<n>` names in all 12 plain files that have blends, counting CTC 02's 2-degree cone blend as a fillet.
